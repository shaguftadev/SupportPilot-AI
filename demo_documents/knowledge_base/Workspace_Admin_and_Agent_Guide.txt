SupportPilot Help Center — Workspace Administration

Owners manage billing, ownership, and workspace-wide settings. Administrators manage members, integrations, and configuration. Agents work tickets within assigned permissions. Exact permissions can vary by plan.

For invitations, confirm email, workspace, and invitation status; resend expired invitations from member management. For ownership transfer, verify the requester and escalate if the current owner is unavailable. Never transfer ownership based only on an email request.

For access problems, confirm workspace membership, role, resource sharing, and the signed-in account before troubleshooting further.
